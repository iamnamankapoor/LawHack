6 · Our solutionSo we built a harness, not another assistant.
Any legal AI runs it behind its answers, and the client only gets what the sources support.

The LLM never decides who is speaking.

Attributing

Jev (TypeSafe) + the Court's drafting rules

Every sentence gets a speaker, a probability, and what the Court did with it. The Court's drafting rules decide first; Jev takes the ambiguous calls.

Once per ruling2.1 s

Answering

Any LLM · Mistral · Claude · GPT

Any model answers from the labelled ruling. Every sentence carries its paragraph and speaker. When the ruling is silent, it says so.

Per questionAny model

Verifying

Jev guard + rewrite

Each sentence is re-checked against its source. Pass, review or blocked, with the paragraph as proof and a rewrite.

Per sentence0.39 s

Rule of the build: what code and rules can decide is never handed to a model. Jev makes typed decisions; the LLM only writes.
