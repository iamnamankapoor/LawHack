Legible

A reading tool for lawyers, not a dashboard. The decision text is the product; everything else stays quiet so that who is speaking is the one thing the eye picks up.

Mark and wordmark

The mark is an L made of the reading margin: its stem is the bar Legible draws beside each paragraph, split into the three voices (Court, cour d'appel, parties), standing on an ink baseline next to lines of text. The wordmark is one word, “Legible”, set in Source Serif 4 Semibold, the same face as the decisions.

Colour: one hue per voice

Radix Colors, light only. Warm sand neutrals; a voice colour is used for the margin bar, a faint tint behind the paragraph and its label. The Court's verdict on a passage uses a separate green / red / amber set, always with an icon and a word, never colour alone.

A paragraph, labelled

Only the paragraphs that are not the Court's carry a label, which says who is speaking and what the Court did with it. The Court's own paragraphs carry no label: they are the default.

Cour d'appel non tranché par la Cour

Il retient, enfin, que le seul fait, non contesté par l'employeur, qu'il s'agit d'un poste sans responsabilité managériale […] ne suffit pas à considérer que cette proposition n'est pas loyale et sérieuse.

En statuant ainsi, alors que le refus de la salariée du seul poste de reclassement qui lui avait été proposé ne suffisait pas à démontrer l'exécution sérieuse et loyale de l'obligation de reclassement […], la cour d'appel a violé le texte susvisé.

Principles

  • Blank first. The app opens on one box: paste the decision or drop a PDF / .txt. An example is one quiet link away.
  • The text stays whole. Legible never rewrites the decision. “La Cour seulement” folds the other voices into one-line placeholders that open in place.
  • Quote, don't paraphrase. The synthesis is the Court's own sentences, verbatim, each linked to its paragraph. Interpretation stays with the lawyer.
  • Show the check. Every answer says whether its attributions were verified; every flagged sentence names its source paragraph and offers a rewrite.
